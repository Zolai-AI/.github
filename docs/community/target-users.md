## Detailed personas

### Persona 1: Diaspora learner (PRIMARY)
- **Age:** 20–40, living outside Myanmar
- **Goal:** Reconnect with heritage language; teach children
- **Current tools:** YouTube videos, WhatsApp family chats, Bible apps
- **Pain:** No dictionary app; no grammar reference; spelling uncertainty
- **Quote (hypothesis):** "I want my kids to read Zolai but I can't teach what I don't have tools for."
- **Validation status:** UNVALIDATED — needs interview (KR5.1)

### Persona 2: In-country student (PRIMARY)
- **Age:** 12–22, Chin State / Myanmar
- **Goal:** School + church literacy; exam prep
- **Current tools:** Printed dictionaries, school textbooks
- **Pain:** No digital learning path; slow lookup
- **Validation status:** UNVALIDATED — needs interview (KR5.1)

### Persona 3: Teacher / community educator (SECONDARY)
- **Age:** 25–60
- **Goal:** Create lesson materials; correct student writing
- **Current tools:** Word docs, printed Zomi Bibles
- **Pain:** No ZVS 2018 spell checker; no exercise generator
- **Validation status:** UNVALIDATED — needs interview (KR5.1)

### Persona 4: Researcher / developer (SECONDARY)
- **Goal:** Build Zolai NLP tools; publish papers
- **Current tools:** Raw JSONL / SQLite
- **Pain:** No gold eval sets; no standard benchmark
- **Validation status:** PARTIAL — AmericasNLP community confirms this pattern
