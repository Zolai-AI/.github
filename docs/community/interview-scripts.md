# Interview Scripts (KR5.1)

**Status:** DRAFT — founder reviews before use.
**Goal:** 5 interviews with native Tedim Zolai speakers (mix in-country + diaspora).
**Target:** Complete by Nov 2026 (Wave 3, Days 5–14).

| Family / church networks | 2 speakers | "I'm building free Zolai learning tools — 30 min of your time would help me make them useful." |

## Script A: Diaspora learner (20–40 min)

### Warm-up (5 min)
1. Where do you live now? Where is your family from?
2. How often do you speak or write Zolai?
3. Who do you speak Zolai with?

### Current tools (10 min)
4. What do you use when you need to look up a Zolai word?
5. Have you tried any language apps or websites? Which? What worked / didn't?
6. How do you check if your spelling is correct (ZVS 2018)?

### Needs (10 min)
7. If you had a perfect Zolai app, what would the FIRST thing you use it for?
8. Do you teach Zolai to your children? How? What's hardest about it?
9. What Zolai content do you wish existed online?

### Closing (5 min)
10. Would you try a free beta? Would you pay for an advanced version?
11. Who else should I talk to? (referral)

---

## Script B: In-country student / young speaker (15–20 min)

### Warm-up (5 min)
1. How old are you? Where do you study/live?
2. How often do you use Zolai — at school, church, home?

### Learning (10 min)
3. How do you learn Zolai? (school, family, church, self-study)
4. What's the hardest part of reading or writing Zolai?
5. Do you use a phone/computer for Zolai? What apps or sites?

### Needs (5 min)
6. If you could have one Zolai tool on your phone, what would it do?
7. Would you use a free vocabulary quiz app? Daily?

---

## Script C: Teacher / community educator (20–25 min)

### Warm-up (5 min)
1. Where do you teach? What ages / levels?
2. What Zolai materials do you currently use?

### Pain points (10 min)
3. How do you check student spelling against ZVS 2018?
4. Do you create your own exercises? How long does that take?
5. What do students most often get wrong?

### Tools (10 min)
6. What would make your teaching easier — dictionary lookup, exercise generator, grammar checker?
7. Would your school / church pay for a class license? What's a fair price?
