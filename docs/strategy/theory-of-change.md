# Theory of Change

```text
Language Data
      ↓
Knowledge Infrastructure
      ↓
Research & Evaluation
      ↓
Language Technology
      ↓
Literacy & Education
      ↓
Community Adoption
      ↓
Measurable Impact
      ↓
Sustainable Products / Funding
      ↓
Long-Term Language Technology Ecosystem
```

For each stage: inputs · activities · outputs · outcomes · impact · assumptions · risks · indicators.

| Stage | Inputs | Activities | Outputs | Outcomes | Impact (aspirational) | Assumptions | Risks | Indicators |
|-------|--------|------------|---------|----------|----------------------|-------------|-------|------------|
| Language Data | Sources, CREDITS, stewards | Ingest, clean, provenance | Canonical tables/datasets | Trustworthy base | — | Licenses allow use | Legal/permission gaps | % sources documented; row audits |
| Knowledge Infrastructure | DB, wiki, embeddings | Schema, RAG index, versioning | Retrievable knowledge brain | Reliable context for tools | — | Data quality sufficient | Drift/duplication | Retrieval smoke tests; SoT matrix |
| Research & Evaluation | Papers, gold sets | Lit review, benchmarks | Methods, baselines | Credibility | Field contribution | Community validation possible | Thin evaluation | Papers read; benchmark coverage |
| Language Technology | NLP stack, MCP, APIs | Build/fix tools | Working features (labeled status) | Useful capability | — | Users can access | Overclaiming features | Implemented vs planned labels |
| Literacy & Education | Curriculum, exercises | Pathways, pilots | Learning resources | Skill gains (measured) | Literacy support | Speakers engage | Low adoption | Pilot completion; interview N |
| Community Adoption | Outreach, consent | Interviews, annotation | Feedback loops | Sustained use | — | Trust & access | Extractive dynamics | Active contributors |
| Measurable Impact | Metrics framework | M&E | Impact reports | Evidence of benefit | Community benefit | Indicators valid | Vanity metrics | Documented KRs |
| Sustainable Products / Funding | Grants, products | Applications, discovery | Revenue/grants (if any) | Continuity | — | Ethics preserved | Premature commercialization | Grant readiness checklist |
| Long-term ecosystem | Org capacity | Partnerships, roles | Institutions & tools | Durable ecosystem | Language tech commons | Succession possible | Founder bottleneck | Org roadmap progress |
