# Mission & Vision

## Project identity (CONFIRMED working identity)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Project | Zolai AI |
| Language focus | Tedim Zolai / Zomi language technology |
| Orthography ground truth | ZVS 2018; SOV; ergative `in` |
| Founder & Technical Lead | Peter Lianpi |
| Strategic Advisor | Shwe Yee (advisory only) |

**What Zolai AI is:** a research and technology initiative building reliable language data, NLP tools, RAG systems, language intelligence, literacy technologies, educational resources, and — when the foundation is reliable enough — specialized AI models and applications for Zomi/Tedim language users.

**Treat as:** Language Technology · AI/NLP Research · Digital Language Preservation · Literacy & Education · Community Development · Open/Responsible Research · Sustainable Technology/Product Development.

**Do not** describe Zolai AI as only an LLM-training project.

**Strategic principle:**

> Build trustworthy language data, knowledge infrastructure, evaluation, research, and community impact first; specialized model training can follow when the foundation is sufficiently reliable.

---

## Mission (what we do now)

Create practical, evidence-based language technology and knowledge infrastructure that help Tedim Zolai (ZVS 2018) speakers and learners access information, learn language, and participate digitally — grounded in verified data, responsible licensing, and community-aware design.

*(Aligned with root README + OKR system; wording refined 2026-09-18 for evidence-based tone.)*

## Vision (long-term enablement)

A durable Zomi language-technology ecosystem: community-informed tools, open research artifacts, measurable literacy impact, and sustainable operations — without exaggerated “first/only/perfect” claims.

## Strategic pillars

| # | Pillar | Focus |
|---|--------|--------|
| 1 | Language Data & Infrastructure | Dictionaries, corpora (where permitted), alignments, syllables, morphology, provenance, evaluation sets |
| 2 | Zolai NLP & AI Research | Tokenization, segmentation, semantics, grammar, translation, embeddings, RAG, evaluation; specialized models later-stage |
| 3 | Literacy & Education | Learning pathways, vocab/grammar, teacher/learner tools |
| 4 | Community & Digital Development | Preservation, accessibility, diaspora/community collaboration; measurable indicators over vague claims |
| 5 | Research & Open Knowledge | Literature, gaps, benchmarks, reports, responsible data practices |
| 6 | Products & Sustainable Business | APIs, apps, services — validate need before forcing commercialization |
| 7 | Partnerships, Grants & Organization | Universities, language orgs, funders, NGOs, contributors |
