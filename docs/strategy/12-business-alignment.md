## Business vs Mission Alignment Check

| Activity | Mission Impact | Revenue Impact | Verdict |
|----------|:--------------:|:--------------:|---------|
| Freemium web app | ✅ High (language learning) | Medium | ✅ Proceed |
| MCP server API | ✅ Medium (AI integration) | Low | ⚠️ Consider |
| Dataset licensing | ✅ High (research impact) | Medium | ✅ Proceed |
| Consulting services | ⚠️ Low (diverts time) | High | ⚠️ Caution |
| Premium features | ✅ Medium (sustains platform) | High | ✅ Proceed |
| Advertising | ❌ Low (distracting) | Low | ❌ Don't do |

**Decision Rule:** Revenue activities must score "High" or "Medium" on mission impact to proceed.
