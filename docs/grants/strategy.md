## Principles (CONFIRMED framing)

1. Evidence and data governance before application polish  
2. Clear founder / advisor roles — no undefined commercial terms in applications  
3. Mark programs `researching` until **current official** eligibility pages are checked  
4. Tie each application to OKRs + theory of change  
5. Never claim suitability without verifying requirements
