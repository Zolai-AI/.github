## Justification notes

- **Personnel:** Solo-founder project; stipend ensures sustained 20–25 hr/week capacity (see `../strategy/gap-register.md` founder section).
- **Linguist consultant:** Gold-set annotation requires L1 Tedim speakers (KR3.3); rate benchmarks at regional researcher consult rates.
- **Compute:** RAG-first strategy (DEC-001) keeps compute minimal — evaluation baselines only, not model training.
- **Direct participant costs:** CARE-aligned — community participants compensated for time (see `../governance/data-governance.md`).
- **Indirect:** 10% is a placeholder — actual rate depends on fiscal sponsor / entity (KR4.5).

## Checklist before submission (KR4.2)

- [ ] Founder fills actual amounts (not ranges)
- [ ] Every line has 1-sentence justification
- [ ] Budget total ≤ program cap
- [ ] Personnel rate justified (hours × rate)
- [ ] Indirect rate matches program policy
- [ ] No double-counting with existing resources (in-kind)
- [ ] Reviewer sign-off (advisor position vacant — founder self-review for now)

## Non-rules

- Do not inflate budget to program cap without justification
- Do not include unsupported line items (reviewers reject these)
- Do not commit founder equity/commercial terms in budget narratives
