## Letter A — Bible editions

**Target(s):** Myanmar Bible Society (S1a) · Zomi Christian Literature Society (S1b) · Alliance Bible Committee (S1c)  
**Send one copy per target, addressed to the rights/permissions contact for each edition.**

> **Subject:** Permission request: Tedim Zolai Bible editions for language learning tools

Dear [Name / Rights and Permissions Team] — [contact TBD],

I am writing on behalf of **Zolai AI**, an open-source, community-rooted research and technology initiative for Tedim Zolai literacy and language preservation. We are led by founder **Peter Pau Sian Lian**, working with Zomi speakers, teachers, and researchers. Our mission is to keep Tedim Zolai readable, teachable, and searchable for the next generation.

**What we have built:** a cleaned, ZVS-2018-aligned language corpus drawn from public sources; a bilingual dictionary app; a RAG-based translation assistant; and a free learning platform (web + offline desktop app) offering word-by-word sentence analysis, vocabulary quizzes, and progressive lessons. The Bible is the only complete, trusted English–Zolai parallel corpus available for our language, which is why it anchors our literacy and grammar examples. We use it strictly as a *language corpus* for teaching — never to publish or distribute the Scriptures as a religious product.

**Our specific request — non-commercial permission to:**
1. Use verse text as language examples inside our learning tools (sentence analysis, quizzes, grammar explanations);
2. Include the parallel text in our internal research corpus for model evaluation and RAG retrieval;
3. Cite verses in our publications, white papers, and dataset documentation.

**What we will NOT do without separate, written permission:** redistribute the full Bible text as a standalone product or downloadable dataset; commercial resale of any edition; or modify, abridge, or restate theological content.

**In return, we offer:** clear attribution in our `CREDITS.md` and on every dataset card, direct links back to your official editions, edition-level credit where you tell us which text we may use, and immediate removal of any passage on request — no questions asked.

If we do not hear from you within **30 days**, we will keep all usage restricted to internal research only and will not use the text in any public product.

Thank you for the work of translating and stewarding this text. We would be glad to answer questions or share a demo of the tools.

With respect,

## Letter C — Grammar / Zomi Sinna rights holders

**Target(s):** *Zolai Grammar Vol 1* (Taang Zomi, 2010) and *Zolai Sinna* (ZAUS, 2010) — author, publisher, or ZAUS rights contact.

> **Subject:** Quotation permission for grammar documentation (RAG indexing)

Dear [Author / Publisher / Rights holder] — [contact TBD],

I am **Peter Pau Sian Lian**, founder of **Zolai AI**, an open-source, community-rooted initiative for Tedim Zolai literacy and language preservation. Your grammar documentation is one of the reference works our team relies on to teach ZVS 2018 orthography correctly.

**What we are asking for — permission to quote short passages**, specifically:

1. **Definitions and rule statements** — short excerpts (typically one sentence per entry) explaining a grammatical category;
2. **Example sentences** — individual examples illustrating a pattern, each quoted as-is with its gloss;
3. **Indexing for RAG retrieval** — storing these short quotations in our retrieval index so learners can ask questions like "how does the ergative `in` work?" and receive a sourced answer with the citation attached.

**What this is not:** we are **not** seeking to republish your book, reproduce full chapters or lesson texts, scan and redistribute pages, or create a competing edition. Quote length stays at roughly **one sentence per entry**, always attributed to your work with title, author/publisher, and year, plus a purchase or contact link so readers can obtain the original.

**What we will NOT do without separate, written permission:** reproduce sustained passages; publish the book's structure, table of contents, or exercises as a whole; or use excerpts in commercial products.

**We offer:** attribution in our `CREDITS.md` and dataset cards, a link back to where the work can be purchased, review of any passage you flag, and immediate removal on request.

If we do not hear from you within **30 days**, we will keep usage limited to internal research and fair-use-style citation in publications — the material will not appear in public products.

Thank you for documenting the language so carefully. I would be glad to answer questions.

With respect,  
[Founder name — Peter Pau Sian Lian]
