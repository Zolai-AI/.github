# Zolai Language Knowledge (for pcore-brain AI context)

## Language: Tedim Zolai (ZVS 2018)
- Tibeto-Burman, Chin branch, spoken by ~700K Zomi people (Myanmar/India)
- SOV word order, ergative marker `in`
- ZVS 2018 orthography standard

## Grammar Rules — CRITICAL

### Word Order (SOV)
- Simple intransitive: Agreement + Verb → `Pai ka hi.` (I go)
- Simple transitive: Object + Agreement + Verb → `Gam ka mu hi.` (I see the land)
- With possessive: POSS + Object + Agreement + Verb → `Ka gam na mu hi.` (You see my land)
- Agreement markers: ka=I, na=you, a=he/she, i=we(incl), ko=we(excl)
- Agreement marker comes DIRECTLY BEFORE the verb

### Negation
- PRIMARY: `kei` — standard for ALL persons → `Ka pai kei hi.` (I don't go)
- SECONDARY: `lo` — literary/formal, 3rd person only, NO `a` agreement → `Pai lo hi.` (Goes not)
- ❌ WRONG: `A pai lo hi.` (lo never takes `a`)
- FUTURE NEGATIVE: `kei + ding` → `Ka pai kei ding hi.` (I won't go)
- COMPOUND: `kei lo` (emphatic negation)
- ASPECTUAL NEGATIVE: `nai lo` (not yet), `nawn lo` (not anymore), `thei kei` (can't), `om kei` (doesn't exist)
- IMPERATIVE NEGATIVE: `kei in` / `kei un` / `kei hen` (don't!)

### Questions
- YES/NO: `hiam` at end → `Na pai hiam?` (Do you go?)
- CONTENT: `bang hang` + verb + subject + `hiam` → `Bang hang pai na hiam?` (Why do you go?)
- Rhetorical: `diam` → `Na lung a kim diam?` (Don't you have a heart?)
- WHAT: `bang` → `Na ne bang hiam?` (What do you eat?)
- WHERE: `kua` → `Na pai kua hiam?` (Where do you go?)

### Tense & Aspect
- PRESENT: `hi` → `A pai hi.` (He goes)
- PAST: `ta` or `ciangin` → `A pai ta hi.` (He went)
- FUTURE: `ding` → `A pai ding hi.` (He will go)
- COMPLETIVE: `zo` or `-sak` → `A pai zo hi.` (He finished going)
- PROGRESSIVE: `lai` → `A ne lai hi.` (He is eating)
- HABITUAL: `pah` → `A ne pah hi.` (He habitually eats)
- EXPERIENTIAL: `khin` → `Ka mu khin hi.` (I have seen before)

### Pronouns
- FREE: Kei=I, Nang=you, Amah=he/she, Kote=we(excl), Ei/I=we(incl), Noteng=you(pl), Amaute=they
- AGREEMENT: ka=I, na=you, a=he/she, i=we(incl), ko=we(excl)
- POSSESSIVE: a + noun → `a pa` (his father); pasian' = of God
- `uh` is PLURAL MARKER, NOT "they" pronoun

### Ergative Construction
- Agent marker: `in` (with proper nouns or full noun phrases)
- `Pasian in vantung leh leitung a piangsak hi.` (God created heaven and earth)
- Optional with agreement markers

### Negation by Person
- 1st person: `Ka pai kei hi.` ✅
- 2nd person: `Na pai kei hi.` ✅
- 3rd person: `A pai kei hi.` ✅ OR `Pai lo hi.` ✅ (literary)
- ALL persons use `kei` as standard

## Forbidden Deprecated Forms (ZVS 2018)
- pathian → **pasian** (God)
- ram → **gam** (earth/land)
- fapa → **tapa** (son/life)
- bawipa → **topa** (lord/master)
- siangpahrang → **kumpipa** (savior)
- cu/cun → **tua** (that/when)
- suah → **suahtakna** (holiness, context-dependent)
- nunnak → **nuntakna** (life)

## Numbers
- 1=khat, 2=nih, 3=thum, 4=li, 5=nga, 6=guk, 7=sagih, 8=giat, 9=kua, 10=sawm
- Tens: sawm+number (sawmnih=20, sawmthum=30)
- Hundreds: za+number (zakhat=100)
- Thousands: tul+number (tulkhat=1000)

## Compound Words (Most Productive)
- lung- (heart): lungdam (joy), lungnopna (happiness), lunggim (patience)
- siam- (holy): siangtho (holy/sacred), khasiangtho (holy spirit)
- ki- (reciprocal): kicinna (relationship), kizawhna (fellowship)
- hon- (honorific): honpa (shepherd), kumpipa (savior)
- vang- (heavenly/mighty): vangliatna (power), vantung (heaven)

## RAG Usage Pattern
When answering Zolai language questions:
1. Look up word in dictionary (ZO→EN or EN→ZO)
2. Check phrase database for multi-word expressions
3. Reference Bible verses for usage examples
4. Apply grammar rules (SOV, kei negation, hiam questions, ding future)
5. Flag ZVS 2018 compliance issues
6. NEVER use deprecated forms: pathian, ram, fapa, bawipa, siangpahrang, cu/cun
